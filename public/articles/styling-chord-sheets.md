# How to Style Your Chord Sheets in Chordly

Chordly lets you choose how your chord sheets look, so chords stand out, sections are easy to find and every song fits the way you read music.
This guide walks through the styling options and how to use them on one chord sheet or all of them.

---

## 1. Opening the Style Options
Open a chord sheet and click the **Style** button (the palette icon) in the **Layout/Style** section at the top of the page.

![](/article_assets/style_button.png)

---

## 2. How Chordly Reads Each Line
Styles apply to three parts of a chord sheet, and Chordly works out which part each line is:

- **Headings** name a section on their own line, like `Verse 1:`, `(Bridge)`, `Chorus x2` or `[Verse 1]`.
- **Chords** contain only chords, like `G D/F# Em7`.
- **Lyrics** are everything else.

Click the **?** button in **Layout/Style** any time for a reminder.

---

## 3. Choosing a Text Size
**Text size** at the top sets the size of your chords and lyrics together. Small, Normal, Large and Larger are available.
Chords and lyrics always share a size so your chords stay above the right words. Headings grow or shrink along with them.

---

## 4. Styling Chords, Lyrics and Headings
Use the **Chords**, **Lyrics** and **Heading** tabs to style each part on its own:

- **Font style**: Normal, Bold, Italic or Bold italic.
- **Colour**: keep the default or pick red, orange, green, blue, purple or grey.
- **Size** (headings only): make headings Large, Larger or Largest so sections are easy to spot.

The preview at the top of each tab shows your choices straight away, before you save.

![](/article_assets/style_modal.png)

Click **Save** to style this chord sheet. Your styles carry over when you download it as a PDF.

![](/article_assets/styled_chord_sheet.png)

---

## 5. Using Your Styles on Every Chord Sheet
Found a look you like? Click **Apply to all sheets** instead of Save. Chordly will:

1. Save the styles on this chord sheet.
2. Use them for every new chord sheet you create.
3. Replace the styles on all your existing chord sheets.

Each chord sheet can still be changed on its own afterwards. Use **Apply to all sheets** again whenever you want to share a new set of styles across your library.

---

## 6. Resetting to the Default Styles
Click **Reset** to put a chord sheet back to Chordly's default styles.
To reset all your chord sheets, reset one and then click **Apply to all sheets**.

---

## 7. Two Columns and Compacting
Two more buttons in **Layout/Style** help a song fit on the screen or page:

- **Two columns** (the columns icon) shows the chord sheet in two columns on larger screens.
- **Compact** (the text height icon) removes extra blank lines, keeping one before each heading to separate sections.

---

## Final Thoughts
A few styling choices go a long way: bold chords, coloured headings and the right text size make a song much quicker to read, whether you're practising at home or playing on stage.
